How the range is built: every uncertain factor above is treated as a Low–Likely–High triangular distribution instead of one fixed number. The calculator draws 6,000 random combinations from those distributions, runs the full pipeline for each, and reports the median plus the middle band as your range. Raw MAL counts are treated as exact — only the assumptions get uncertainty, not the numbers you looked up. Parameters are sampled independently, which slightly understates the true uncertainty if some of these actually move together in reality. And the range is only as honest as the Low/High you set — narrowing them will always shrink the interval whether or not that's justified.
